GreekFit Terms of Use and Community Guidelines

Version 2026-10-23 · Operator: Yashwanth Reddy Medapati · Contact: support.greekfit@gmail.com

Who can use GreekFit

You can log workouts without an account. To create an account or use community features (public profiles, following, publishing, and groups) you must be at least 13 years old, or older if the law where you live requires it. If you are under 18, use GreekFit with a parent's or guardian's permission. Where the App Store or Google Play provides an age signal required by law, GreekFit may use it to turn off community features for accounts that don't meet the minimum age.

You're responsible for keeping your account secure and for activity on it.

Community Guidelines

  • No harassment, bullying, threats, or hate speech.
  • No nudity, sexual content, or anything that sexualizes minors.
  • No dangerous training, drug, or medical advice presented as fact.
  • No spam, scams, impersonation, or sharing other people's private information.
  • Only post content you have the right to share.
  • Don't use GreekFit to break the law, attack the service, or collect other people's data.

Child safety. GreekFit has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material. We remove it, suspend the accounts involved, and report it to the appropriate authorities (in the U.S., the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children). Report child-safety concerns in the app or to [CHILD SAFETY CONTACT EMAIL].

Your content

You own what you post. By publishing a workout or routine, or posting in a group (including chat messages and a group photo), you let GreekFit store and show it to the people who can see it, and let other users copy published workouts and routines into their own GreekFit routines.

Unpublishing or deleting content removes it from GreekFit, but copies other people already saved stay with them.

Reporting, moderation, and enforcement

You can report any profile, published item, or group post, and block any account. Reports are reviewed by the GreekFit team, which aims to act on them within [TARGET RESPONSE TIME, e.g. 24 hours]. Content reported by several people may be hidden automatically until it's reviewed.

We may remove content, hide profiles, limit features, or suspend or delete accounts that break these terms. Automated limits also stop spam, such as too many follows or posts in a short time.

Health and safety

GreekFit is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. BMI, calorie estimates, exercise instructions, and other people's workouts are general information, not medical or professional advice. Talk to a doctor before starting a new exercise program, especially if you have a health condition, are pregnant, or are recovering from an injury. Stop exercising if you feel pain, dizziness, or shortness of breath.

Hercules, the AI coach

Hercules writes training suggestions with an outside AI service (Groq). Its answers can be wrong or not right for you: check them against how you feel and your own judgment, and don't treat them as medical advice. Hercules has daily and monthly use limits, and we may change or remove it.
