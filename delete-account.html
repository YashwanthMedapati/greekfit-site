Delete your GreekFit account

In the app, open Profile > Settings > Cloud Backup > Delete account, review what will be removed, and type DELETE. Your account, cloud backup, public profile and profile photo, follows, blocks, published workouts and routines, comments and kudos, group memberships, chat messages, posts, votes, groups you own and their group photos, workout buddies, shared streaks and high-fives, and your Hercules usage counts will be deleted. GreekFit also clears its saved training records on that device.

If you no longer have the app, request account and associated-data deletion by emailing support.greekfit@gmail.com from the email address linked to your GreekFit account. The operator must verify the request before deletion. Do not send your password, measurements, or other sensitive workout records by email.

Copies of your published workouts or routines that other people already saved to their own devices, past exercise issue reports without an account ID, provider backups and security logs for their documented retention period, and copies of exported files you saved elsewhere may remain. See the privacy policy for details.
