Common questions

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open Profile → Settings → Cloud Backup → Delete account, review what will be removed and type DELETE. Your account and its cloud data are deleted. No longer have the app? See Delete your account.

Do I need an account?

Yes. An account keeps your workouts backed up and lets you train with friends. Once you're signed in, logging works offline too, and everything syncs when you're back online.

How do I get my workouts out of GreekFit?

Open Profile → Settings → Your Data and choose Export Workout History (CSV) for a spreadsheet, or Export Workout Records (JSON) for a full backup you can import again.

Can I bring my history from another app?

Yes. In Profile → Settings → Your Data, choose Import from Strong or Hevy and pick the CSV file you exported from that app.

I signed up but didn't get a verification code.

Check your spam folder, then tap Resend email on the code screen. If that email already has a GreekFit account, no code is sent: sign in instead, the way you created it (password, Google or Apple).

What does Hercules, the AI coach, do with my data?

Nothing until you agree on his first screen. After that, he only receives what's needed to answer the question you ask. You can choose whether he sees your body data. See the privacy policy.

Someone is bothering me in a group or comments.

Open their profile or the post and use Report or Block. Blocked people can't see your profile or posts, and reports go to our moderators.
